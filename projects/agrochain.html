WORK / 04 / AGRI-TECH

AgroChain.

A connected concept combining farmer-side crop insights with marketplace access.

TEAM CONCEPT / PROTOTYPEAGRI-TECH · HACKATHON CONCEPT · PRODUCT THINKING
GitHub repository ↗
✳
FIELD → MARKET

01 / THE PROBLEM

Start with a clear question.

The concept explored how fragmented crop information and market access affect farmers and local vendors. AgroChain brought together the farmer-facing Fasal Mitra idea and the supplier marketplace direction of SupplySathi.

02 / DESIGN DECISIONS

Two connected journeys

Separate farmer-side crop insights from vendor/supplier discovery while keeping them part of one ecosystem.

Accessibility

Consider multilingual interaction and voice input for users who may not prefer typing.

Information design

Use dashboards to surface prices, market context, and crop insights rather than forcing users to search through unstructured text.

Prototype boundary

This is a concept/prototype story; the portfolio does not claim a deployed production marketplace.

WHAT I TOOK AWAY

  • ✳A product concept gets clearer when user journeys are written separately.
  • ✳Language and accessibility are core product decisions, not decorations.
  • ✳A hackathon demo should make scope and unbuilt pieces obvious.

03 / NEXT ITERATION

What I’d improve next.

Validate the workflow with intended users, narrow the first release, and test a small end-to-end journey.
